# Fair Dinkum

A pickleball session organiser: fair court, partner and sit-out rotation, optional scoring with a leaderboard, a player and group list, and scheduled sessions.

**Open the app:** https://bunglebogs.github.io/fair-dinkum/

## Your data stays on your device

Fair Dinkum has no server and sends nothing anywhere. Player names, groups, sessions and scores are kept on the device you use it on:

- In Edge or Chrome on a computer, you can choose a folder for the player database (`players.json`), with automatic backups.
- Otherwise the data is kept in your browser's storage. Use **Export backup** in *Players & groups* to keep a copy.

Each person who uses the link has their own, separate data.
