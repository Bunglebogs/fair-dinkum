Fair Dinkum – Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Summary

Everything you enter in Fair Dinkum stays on the device you use it on, unless the organiser turns on online sign-ups. In that case a small amount of information about published sessions is stored online, in the EU, so players can say whether they're coming and get emails (see below). Nothing is used for advertising or tracking.

What the app stores

To organise sessions, the app stores the information you type in: player names, groups, scheduled sessions, court names, settings and match scores. This is kept only on your device, either:

  • in a file in a folder you choose (players.json, plus dated copies in a Backups subfolder), or
  • in your browser or app's own storage on the device.

The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Apart from online sign-ups (below), it does not send any of your information to the developer or to anyone else.

Online sign-ups (optional)

If an organiser connects the app to online sign-ups and publishes a session, the following is stored on a server run with Cloudflare, in a database restricted to the European Union:

  • the session's name, date and time;
  • the short names (for example “Paul C”) of the players invited, and the name typed by anyone who adds themselves;
  • each player's answer (coming or not) and when they gave it;
  • an email address, only if a player types their own and ticks to agree to emails.

Anyone with a session's sign-up link can see its list of short names and answers. Email addresses are never shown, and are used only to send that group's invites, a confirmation when a player signs up, and a reminder before the session. Emails are sent through Brevo, whose servers are in the EU.

How long it's kept: a session and its answers are deleted when the organiser finishes or deletes it, or 30 days after it takes place. An email address is kept until the player clicks the link at the bottom of any email, which deletes it straight away.

Who is responsible: the organiser who publishes the session (usually on behalf of a club or group) decides what is published and is responsible for it. Players can ask their organiser to remove their name or answers.

Who is responsible for the data

Because the data never leaves your device, you (or the club or group you organise play for) are responsible for it. If you record other people's names, let them know they are being recorded to organise play and scores, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Removing your data

You can delete individual players, groups or everything from within the app (Players & groups). If you chose a folder, you can also delete players.json and the Backups folder. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data removes anything held in app or browser storage.

Hosting

The app's files are hosted on GitHub Pages. When the app is loaded, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address, as it does for any website; see GitHub's privacy statement. None of the information you enter into the app is sent to GitHub.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the app's GitHub issues page.
